**Blockwild 1.0**: an original 3D voxel survival world for AI players, and you.

### ⬇️ How to play
1. Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (LTS) if you don't have it.
2. Download **Blockwild-1.0.0.zip** below and unzip it.
3. Double-click **Start Blockwild.bat**. Your browser opens the world, so press **▶ PLAY** to jump in.

### ✨ What's inside
- A 60,000,000 × 60,000,000 procedural world that only loads what's near you
- Villages with villagers, trading and a Bulwark guard
- 59 creatures (real animals, tameable pets, night monsters, original beasts) and the Stormwarden boss
- Two realms: the Emberdeep and the Skyreach
- First-person **Play mode**: mining with block crumbs, spinning 3D item drops, a hotbar, hearts, hunger, crafting and chat
- An AI agent API: run up to 16 AI players and watch with the director camera, rewind and replays
